About our data

Sources

Information should come only from banks’ official product pages, fee schedules, RBI notices and similar public documents. Each record should show its source link and the date we last checked it.

Current status

Rates, fees and limits for savings, fixed and recurring deposits, home, personal, gold, education and car loans, UPI and sample credit cards were gathered on 29 Oct 2026 from free public sources such as Zee Business, Business Today, Paisabazaar, BankerMart, 5paisa, ClearTax and The Financial Economy. Some source pages are older than the snapshot date, and the “Last checked” row says which. They are third-party summaries, not the banks’ own rate cards, and sources sometimes disagree (home loans most of all), so we show ranges. This page cannot pull live feeds, so it shows a dated snapshot. Every other product still reads “Not verified”. Bank websites remain the final word.

Which lenders are covered

We list all 12 public sector banks, all 21 private sector banks and 11 small finance banks from RBI’s list of scheduled commercial banks, plus selected NBFCs and housing finance companies. Foreign banks, regional rural banks and co-operative banks are not listed individually; see RBI’s list from the All lenders page. Most lenders have no verified figures yet and show “Not verified”.

How comparison works

Every product uses the same rows so banks line up. Missing data stays blank and is labelled. Banks are listed alphabetically, never ranked by commission.

How we handle your answers

Quiz answers stay in this browser page while you use it. We do not send them anywhere, and you can review or clear them at any time. Saved comparisons are stored only in your own browser, if storage is available.

Advertising and referrals

No referral or advertising relationships are active. If any are added, they will be disclosed on the relevant page. Paid placement will not change quiz suitability results.

Documents lists

The basic KYC list (one officially valid document plus PAN or Form 60) follows RBI’s KYC rules and FAQ. Extra documents for each product are typical requests, not rules. Every lender can ask for more or less, so check with them before you go.

Not advice

Results are general information, not personal financial advice and not a promise of approval. Always confirm current terms with the bank.
